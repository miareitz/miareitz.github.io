Mia Reitz is a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Kassel, where she works in the Programming Languages and Methodologies group. She holds a Ph.D. from the University of Kassel, completed in 2025, where she also earned her bachelor's and master's degrees.

Her research focuses on high-performance computing (HPC) and asynchronous many-task (AMT) programming, with an emphasis on fault tolerance and load balancing.

She occasionally teaches introductory courses in computer science and parallel programming. If you are interested in what she has been up to, please have a look at her <a href="/projects/">Personal Projects</a>, her <a href="/publications/">Publications</a>, and her <a href="/cv/">CV</a>.
